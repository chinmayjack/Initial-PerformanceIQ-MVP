# PerformanceIQ Pilot Outreach

## Ideal First Customers

- 50-500 employee companies
- Managers struggle with review quality and consistency
- HR team owns review cycles but lacks clean evidence trails
- Current process uses spreadsheets, docs, Slack feedback, and Workday/Lattice exports

## One-Sentence Pitch

PerformanceIQ helps managers write fair, evidence-backed performance reviews in minutes using employee goals, feedback, growth history, and AI-generated review drafts.

## Discovery Questions

1. How do managers currently gather evidence for performance reviews?
2. How long does one manager review usually take?
3. Where do goals, feedback, promotion notes, and calibration data live today?
4. What causes the most friction during review cycles?
5. How do you identify promotion-ready or at-risk employees before calibration?
6. What would make this trustworthy enough for managers and HR to use?

## Pilot Offer

We are opening a limited pilot for teams that want to reduce review-cycle time and improve review fairness. The pilot includes:

- Tenant-safe workspace
- Manager dashboard
- Employee profiles
- Goal and feedback tracking
- AI review draft generation
- HR analytics dashboard
- Weekly onboarding and feedback session

Suggested pilot length: 30 days.

Suggested pilot price: free for 2 design partners, then $1,500-$5,000 for paid pilots depending on company size.

## Demo Flow

1. Open the landing page and explain the review-cycle pain.
2. Open Dashboard and show manager visibility.
3. Open Employees and show performance profiles.
4. Open Reviews and generate an AI review draft.
5. Open Workspace and show tenant isolation/readiness.
6. Ask which workflow would be valuable enough for a pilot.

## Pilot Success Metrics

- Review draft time reduced by at least 50%
- Managers use AI draft as a starting point, not a final answer
- HR reports better evidence quality during calibration
- At least one department asks to expand beyond the pilot group
